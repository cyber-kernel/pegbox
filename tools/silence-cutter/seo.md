# Silence Cutter Online — Automatic Video & Audio Jump-Cuts

## Automatically Cut Pauses, Gaps, and Dead Air in Your Browser
Pegbox Silence Cutter (also known as **Cut**) is an automated jump-cut utility for YouTubers, podcasters, interviewers, and online educators. It scans your media file, locates moments of silence where no one is speaking, and cuts them out seamlessly.

---

## Why Use Pegbox Silence Cutter?

### 1. 100% Client-Side Privacy
Unlike cloud video editing services that upload gigabytes of your personal or proprietary video files, Silence Cutter operates **strictly inside your local web browser** using WebAssembly. Your files are processed entirely in memory on your device.

### 2. Natural Dialogue Padding (No Chopped Words)
A common problem with rough silence removers is that they clip the initial consonant of a word or cut off the tail of a sentence. Silence Cutter automatically applies configurable **breathing room padding** (e.g. 0.15s) around every spoken segment to maintain a natural, engaging human cadence.

### 3. Preserves Audio & Video Formats
- **Audio Files**: MP3 files remain high-quality MP3s; WAV files remain lossless WAVs.
- **Video Files**: MP4, MOV, MKV, and WEBM videos are reconstructed with frame-accurate cuts and fast-start web streaming flags.

---

## Preset Breakdown
- **Gentle (1.0s gap, 0.25s padding)**: Perfect for podcasts, long interviews, and educational lectures where natural thoughtful pauses should remain intact.
- **Balanced (0.5s gap, 0.15s padding)**: The standard recommendation for YouTube videos, vlogs, and screencasts.
- **Aggressive (0.3s gap, 0.08s padding)**: Optimized for fast-paced TikToks, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts where every millisecond counts.

---

## Recommended Workflow: The Pegbox Chain
If your recording has noticeable air conditioner hum or background fan noise, the silence detection algorithm may perceive the background noise as continuous speech.

**Pro-Tip**: Run your file through **Voice Cleaner (Hush)** first. Once cleaned, click **"Continue with Silence Cutter"** on the result screen to pass the cleaned file directly in memory without re-uploading!
